# Interview guide

45 minutes, one stakeholder, two interviewers (one asks, one writes). Used for all 14 discovery interviews. The template version, without Wendelkaai specifics, is in the [playbook](../09-playbook/templates/interview-guide-template.md).

**Before:** read the stakeholder's org chart position and any numbers they own. Send the three questions marked ★ a day ahead.

## 1. Opening (5 min)

- Why we are here: to understand the work before anyone proposes AI. Nothing said is attributed by name in the report unless agreed.
- "What does a good week look like in your team? And a bad one?"

## 2. The work as it is (15 min)

- ★ "Walk me through the last time [core process] went wrong. What happened, who noticed, what did it cost?"
- "Where does your team spend time on work that a customer would not pay for?"
- "Which numbers do you look at every week? Which ones do you wish you had?"
- "Where does information arrive in a form you have to retype, reformat or look up?"
- "Who do new people ask when they are stuck? How long until they stop asking?"

*Listen for:* volumes, handling times, error costs, workarounds (Excel, personal notes), people who are single points of knowledge.

## 3. AI today (10 min)

- ★ "Which AI tools do you or your team use today, officially or not? For what?"
- "What has worked, what disappointed?"
- "What would you never let a machine do in your area? Why?"
- "If AI took over part of your team's work, what would you want them to do instead?"

*Listen for:* shadow AI, data that leaves the company, fears (jobs, blame, control), lines that must not be crossed.

## 4. Constraints (10 min)

- "Which rules, contracts or audits limit what we can do with your data?"
- "Which systems would a solution have to talk to? Who owns them?"
- ★ "If we proposed something for your team, what would you need to see before you trusted it?"

*Listen for:* GDPR, customs and AEO requirements, ISO 27001 scope, customer contract clauses, vendor lock-in, acceptance criteria in their own words.

## 5. Close (5 min)

- "Who else should we talk to? Who disagrees with you?"
- "Can we have one example document, report or e-mail from your area?"
- Confirm what happens next and when they will see the synthesis.

## After

Within 24 hours: notes into the synthesis template (pain point, volume, cost, current workaround, quote, constraint, idea). Tag every number with its source: *measured*, *estimate by interviewee*, or *our assumption*.
