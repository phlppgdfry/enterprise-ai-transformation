Readiness

Ready to use AI, not yet to govern it: 1.9 of 5

  • Strong enough: documents are digital, Microsoft 365 and Entra ID in place, ISO/IEC 27001 certified.
  • Missing: AI policy, AI inventory, an owner for AI, a platform to run it on, labelled data to test it.
  • Lowest: People & skills (1.6). 61% of staff expect AI to take jobs at the desk.
  • Target in 12 months: 3.5, paid for by roadmap items, not wishes.
30 items scored on evidence by the consultant and the COO3
Where to start

Ten use cases scored; documents come first under every weighting

  • UC-01 Freight document intelligence ranks first in 100% of 2,000 re-weightings of the criteria.
  • Next: carrier invoice audit and customer e-mail triage reuse what UC-01 builds.
  • Not generative AI: route optimisation is a solver, and a buy decision.
  • Parked: performance scoring of drivers and pickers: high-risk under the EU AI Act, no owner.
Criteria and weights agreed with the leadership team (45 / 35 / 20)4
The pilot

Reading documents is easy; the clerks' checks make it safe

  • Extraction alone: 36 of 220 documents into the TMS with an error.
  • The clerks' own checks, written as 14 rules, take that to 0.
  • Three rules compare documents of one shipment (weights, package counts, containers). They catch what a confident model gets wrong.
  • The first version failed its gate; the evaluation found the gap in the linking logic.

What it is worth

€189k over three years on the cash view; payback 15 months

  • One-off €85k, about €20k a year. Model usage is about €0.020 per document.
  • Cash view: only hires avoided and leavers not replaced count. Capacity view: €232k.
  • Biggest lever: whether leavers are replaced, then what an error really costs. Not the model.
  • All leavers replaced? Still pays back in 22 months.
  • Everything wrong at once: NPV -€142k. The gates watch those drivers.
8% discount rate; pilot cost (€38k) excluded as sunk7
How we get there

Twelve months, five gates; only the first is asked today

G2 week 13: ≥ 60% straight-through and ≤ 1% escapes on real documents, or we stop8
People and governance

Nobody surprised, nothing high-risk, governance before go-live

Commitments to staff

  • No redundancies because of AI in this roadmap
  • Freed time goes to customs files and exceptions
  • Every second leaver not replaced while it scales
  • The review queue never evaluates individuals
  • Works council informed in week 1, consulted at weeks 14 and 30

Governance in the first 90 days

  • One-page AI policy and one sanctioned assistant
  • AI inventory, every system classified under the EU AI Act
  • AI vendors assessed inside the ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS
  • AI literacy for all staff (AI Act Art. 4)
  • Human oversight designed in: a person sees every doubt
UC-08 (worker performance scoring) parked: Annex III 4(b) high-risk9
The decision

We ask the board for three things

  1. Approve UC-01 production and the year-1 foundations: €85k one-off, about €20k a year, released at gate G2.
  2. Endorse the people commitments.
  3. Mandate the AI steering group (CEO, CFO, COO, IT manager) to take gates G1-G3 within this envelope.

Wave 2 (carrier invoice audit, customer e-mail triage) comes back in week 26 with its own business case.
