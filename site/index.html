Reading documents is the easy part

Extraction alone would have sent 36 of 220 documents into the TMS with an error. The clerks' own checks, written down as 14 rules (three compare documents of one shipment), took that to 0.

The biggest lever is a people decision

The business case moves more with whether leavers are replaced and what an error really costs than with the model's straight-through rate. Freed hours are not saved euros until someone decides what they are for.

Ready to use AI, not to govern it

Digital documents and a certified ISMS, but no AI policy, no inventory and 38% shadow AI. Governance items come first in the roadmap, and performance scoring of workers is parked as high-risk under the EU AI Act.
