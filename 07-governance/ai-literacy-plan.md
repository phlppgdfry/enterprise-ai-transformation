# AI literacy plan

Three tiers, matched to what people do with AI. It meets the AI Act's literacy duty (Art. 4, as amended by the Digital Omnibus: take measures to support AI literacy) and, more importantly, it is the change programme for the 61% who expect AI to take their job and the 38% who already use it without rules.

| Tier | Who | Format | Content | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Everyone | All 250 staff | 90 minutes, per team, in Dutch, French or English on request | What AI is and is not; live demo on Wendelkaai documents; the AI policy in practice; what to do and not do with customer data; where to ask | 90% attended by week 20 |
| 2. Users | Doc desk, customer service, customs, sales, champions | Half a day, hands-on | Working with the review queue or the approved assistant; checking AI output; automation bias; reporting problems | Before go-live of their use case |
| 3. Builders | IT team, internal developer, champions who want it | Two days + pairing | Orchestration layer, prompts as code, evaluation and release gate, security of LLM applications, cost | Before they change anything in production |

## Measuring it

- Attendance per tier, per team.
- Pulse survey before and after: confidence (baseline 22%), use of unapproved tools (baseline 38%).
- For tier 2: share of flagged fields that are actually checked (audit), not just confirmed.

## Records

HR keeps attendance records per person and tier. That is enough to show the measures taken; the amended Art. 4 does not require guaranteeing a level for any individual.
