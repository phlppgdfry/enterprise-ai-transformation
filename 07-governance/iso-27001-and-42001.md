# ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 42001

Wendelkaai is certified to **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** for IT services and customs data. AI does not need a separate management system to start with: it needs to be brought into the existing ISMS. **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** (AI management systems) is used as a checklist now and a certification option later, if customers ask for it.

## AI in the ISMS: what changes

| ISMS element | Change for AI | Roadmap |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Add the document pipeline and the approved AI assistant to the scope statement | R18 |
| Risk assessment | Add AI risks from the [AI risk register](ai-risk-register.md) to the information security risk register | R18 |
| Statement of Applicability | Re-assess the controls below for the AI systems | R18 |
| Supplier management | Assess model and AI SaaS vendors like any cloud supplier, with AI-specific questions | R18 |
| Internal audit | Next audit cycle samples the AI controls | Year 1 audit plan |

## Annex A controls that carry most of the weight (ISO/IEC 27001:2022)

| Control | What it means for AI at Wendelkaai |
|---|---|
| A.5.9 Inventory of information and other associated assets | AI systems, models, prompts and evaluation sets in the asset inventory (shared with the AI inventory) |
| A.5.10 Acceptable use of information | The [AI policy](ai-policy.md) lines 1-2 |
| A.5.19 / A.5.20 Supplier relationships and agreements | Vendor terms: no training on our inputs, retention, processing region, notice of model changes, incident notification |
| A.5.23 Information security for use of cloud services | Model API and AI assistant treated as cloud services; configuration reviewed |
| A.5.34 Privacy and protection of PII | Data minimisation in prompts; DPIA for UC-02 |
| A.6.3 Awareness, education and training | AI literacy tiers 1-3 |
| A.8.12 Data leakage prevention | DLP rule on unapproved chatbot domains |
| A.8.15 / A.8.16 Logging and monitoring | Per-document audit log; escape rate and cost monitoring |
| A.8.25 / A.8.26 / A.8.28 Secure development, application security requirements, secure coding | Prompt and code review, schema-constrained output, untrusted-input handling, evaluation gate in CI |
| A.8.32 Change management | Any change to model, prompt, rule or threshold passes the release gate first |

## Vendor questions for AI suppliers (added to the supplier questionnaire)

1. Are our inputs or outputs used to train or improve your models? Can we opt out contractually?
2. How long are inputs and outputs retained, where, and who can access them?
3. In which regions is data processed? Can we restrict it?
4. How do you notify us of model changes or deprecations, and how long do old versions remain available?
5. Which certifications cover the service (ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2, ISO/IEC 42001)?
6. How do you handle security incidents involving customer data, and within what time do you notify?
7. For general-purpose AI models: where is your documentation under the EU AI Act available?

## ISO/IEC 42001 as a checklist

| 42001 clause | Where Wendelkaai stands after year 1 |
|---|---|
| 4 Context, 5 Leadership | AI policy, steering, roles ([operating model](../05-transformation-roadmap/operating-model.md)) |
| 6.1.2-6.1.3 AI risk assessment and treatment | AI risk register, reviewed at every gate |
| 6.1.4 AI system impact assessment | Per use case at registration: AI Act class, people affected, works-council check |
| 7 Support (competence, awareness) | Literacy programme, champions |
| 8 Operation | Evaluation gate, human oversight design, change management |
| 9 Performance evaluation | KPI dashboard, audit sample, internal audit |
| 10 Improvement | Monday retro, gate reviews, yearly readiness re-assessment |

Certification is a year-2 decision: worth it when two or more customers require it, or when AI systems with higher risk are added.
