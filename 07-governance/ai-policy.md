# AI policy (draft for approval at gate G1)

*One page on purpose: a policy people read beats a complete one nobody does. Owner: CEO. Maintained by: DPO. Review: yearly and whenever the AI inventory adds a high-risk system.*

---

**Wendelkaai Logistics: how we use AI**

**Why.** AI helps us handle more work with the people we have, with fewer errors. We use it in a way our customers, our staff and the law can trust.

**1. Use approved tools.** For general work (writing, summarising, translating, questions) use the approved assistant inside Microsoft 365. The list of approved tools is on the intranet. Public chatbots and browser plug-ins that are not on the list may not be used for work.

**2. Protect data.** Never put the following into a tool that is not approved: customer or shipment data, personal data (names, e-mail addresses, licence plates linked to a person), prices and contracts, customs data, passwords. In approved tools, use only the data the task needs.

**3. A person stays responsible.** AI proposes; people decide and remain accountable for what is sent, declared or paid. Check AI output before you use it, as you would check a new colleague's work. If you are asked to check something, check it.

**4. Be open about it.** Tell customers when they are dealing with an AI system rather than a person. Do not present AI-generated content as a personal statement if it is not.

**5. No AI that judges people without a decision from the board.** We do not use AI to monitor, score or evaluate the performance or behaviour of individual employees, or to make decisions about hiring, tasks or pay, unless the board has decided it after a legal assessment and works-council consultation.

**6. New AI use is registered first.** Every new AI system or use (including a new feature in an existing tool) is registered with the DPO before use, so it can be classified under the EU AI Act and checked for security and privacy. Ask an AI champion if you are unsure.

**7. Learn the basics.** Everyone follows the AI literacy session. People who work with an AI system daily or build one get additional training.

**8. Report problems.** If an AI system gives a wrong, strange or harmful result, or if you think data went where it should not, report it through the IT service desk like any other incident. Nobody is blamed for reporting.
