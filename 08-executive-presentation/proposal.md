# Proposal: from pilot to production

**To:** Board of Wendelkaai Logistics NV · **From:** AI Transformation team · **Date:** 21 October 2026 · **Decision requested:** go for UC-01 production and year-1 foundations

## The situation

Eleven clerks key about 4,000 freight documents a month by hand. 2.1% carry an error that surfaces later as rework, customs amendments or demurrage. Two vacancies have been open for eight months while volume grows about 4% a year. Meanwhile 38% of staff use public chatbots for work, some with customer data, and two pharma customers asked how Wendelkaai governs AI.

## What we did in twelve weeks

1. Assessed AI readiness: <!--n:rd.overall-->1.9<!--/n--> of 5 (<!--n:rd.level-->Experimenting<!--/n-->). Ready to use AI, not yet to govern it.
2. Scored ten use cases with the leadership team. Freight document intelligence ranks first under every weighting we tried.
3. Built and measured a working pilot: on 220 held-out documents <!--n:pilot.stp-->70.0%<!--/n--> go straight to the TMS, <!--n:pilot.escapes-->0<!--/n--> with an unflagged error, and every genuine document problem reaches a person.
4. Built the business case with finance: cash payback <!--n:bc.cash.payback-->15 months<!--/n-->, 3-year NPV <!--n:bc.cash.npv-->€189k<!--/n-->.
5. Classified everything under the EU AI Act. Nothing in the plan is high-risk; one idea (performance scoring of workers) is parked.

## What we propose

| | Scope | Investment | Decision point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Now** | UC-01 to production (all five document types), orchestration layer v1, AI policy and inventory, literacy tier 1-2, works-council track | <!--n:bc.one_off-->€85k<!--/n--> one-off, <!--n:bc.run_cost_y1-->€20k<!--/n--> a year | Gate G2 (week 13): straight-through ≥ 60% and escapes ≤ 1% on real documents |

| Week 39 | Wave 3 discovery: customs classification (UC-03), route optimisation buy decision (UC-07) | €30-40k | Gate G4 |

Only the first row is asked today.

## Why it is safe to say yes

- **Measured, not promised.** The pilot passed a release gate the business set before the build; it goes live only after it passes again on real documents in shadow mode.
- **Small downside, staged.** Year-1 exposure is <!--n:bc.one_off-->€85k<!--/n--> plus run cost, released gate by gate, with kill criteria agreed with the CFO.
- **People first.** No redundancies; every second leaver not replaced while it scales; the works council informed in week 1; the review queue never used to evaluate individuals.
- **Governed from day one.** AI policy, inventory, ISO/IEC 27001 supplier assessment and human-oversight rules come before go-live, not after.

## What we need from the board

1. Approve the UC-01 production budget and the year-1 foundations.
2. Endorse the people commitments (jobs, attrition, no individual monitoring).
3. Mandate the AI steering group (CEO, CFO, COO, IT manager) to take gate decisions G1-G3 within this envelope.
