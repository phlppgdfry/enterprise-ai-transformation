# Five-minute walkthrough for the CEO (script)

A recorded walkthrough in which the consultant presents the findings to the (fictional) CEO, using the deck and the live review queue. Recording it is on the candidate's list; this is the script. Target: 5:00, spoken at about 140 words a minute.

| Time | Screen | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Deck slide 1 | "Ilse, you asked three things: where to start, what it is worth, and how to do it without getting into trouble. Five minutes, three answers." |
| 0:15 | Slide 2: the problem | "Eleven people retype four thousand documents a month. We measured it: five and a half minutes each, and two in a hundred have an error that costs you later. That is the problem we solved first, because it ranked first whichever way your team weighted the criteria." |
| 0:45 | Slide 3: readiness | "Your company is ready to use AI, not yet to govern it. Documents are digital, the ISMS is certified, but there is no AI policy and a third of your people use public chatbots with customer data. That is the cheapest thing to fix, and it starts in week one." |
| 1:15 | Switch to the review queue demo, tour mode | "This is the pilot running on 220 test documents it never saw during tuning. Seven in ten go straight to the TMS. Here is one it stopped: the packing list says 6,943 kilos, the bill of lading 7,466. A clerk sees the reason in plain words and checks two fields, not the whole document." |
| 2:00 | Demo: threshold slider | "This slider is the trade-off your COO set in workshop three: at most one error in a hundred may slip through unseen. Today, by hand, it is two in a hundred. On the test set, nothing slipped through. On the tuning set, two did, both truck documents with a misread digit; the production build adds a check against the booking for exactly that." |
| 2:45 | Slide: what each layer buys | "Why not just 'let AI read the documents'? Because that would have put 36 wrong documents into your TMS out of 220. The business rules your clerks apply in their heads are what makes it safe. We wrote them down and made them run." |
| 3:15 | Slide: business case | "Your CFO's version: fifteen months to pay back, a hundred and ninety thousand euros over three years, on the cash view. The biggest lever is not the AI: it is whether you decide not to replace every second leaver. If you replace all of them, it still pays back, in about two years." |
| 3:50 | Slide: roadmap | "Twelve months, five gates. You only decide on the first one today: eighty-five thousand euros. If shadow mode on real documents misses the bar in week thirteen, we stop." |
| 4:20 | Slide: people and risk | "Your works council heard this plan before anyone else. No redundancies, no monitoring of individuals, and the one idea that would have been high-risk under the AI Act is parked, in writing." |
| 4:45 | Slide: the ask | "So: approve the first stage, endorse the people commitments, and let the steering group take the gates. I'm happy to take questions." |

**Tips for the recording:** one take per section is fine; keep the cursor on what you talk about; show the threshold slider moving and the escape count staying at zero below the budget; end on the ask, not on a thank-you slide.
