# Template: discovery interview guide

45 minutes · one stakeholder · two interviewers (one asks, one writes) · send ★ questions a day ahead.

1. **Opening (5 min).** Purpose: understand the work before proposing anything. Confidentiality. "What does a good week look like? A bad one?"
2. **The work (15 min).** ★ "Walk me through the last time [core process] went wrong." · "Where does your team do work a customer would not pay for?" · "Which numbers do you look at weekly? Which do you wish you had?" · "Where do you retype, reformat or look things up?" · "Who do new people ask?"
3. **AI today (10 min).** ★ "Which AI tools do you use, officially or not?" · "What worked, what disappointed?" · "What would you never let a machine do here?" · "If part of the work disappeared, what should your team do instead?"
4. **Constraints (10 min).** "Which rules, contracts or audits limit what we can do with your data?" · "Which systems would a solution touch, and who owns them?" · ★ "What would you need to see before you trusted it?"
5. **Close (5 min).** "Who else should we talk to? Who disagrees with you?" · Ask for one example document. · Next steps.

**After (within 24h):** pain point · volume · cost · workaround · quote · constraint · idea. Tag each number: *measured* / *interviewee estimate* / *our assumption*.
