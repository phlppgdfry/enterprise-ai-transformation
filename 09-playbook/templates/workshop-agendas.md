# Template: workshop agendas

| # | Workshop | Length | Participants | Decision it must produce |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problems worth solving | 3h | Leadership + 6 people from the floor | Long list of problems, clustered into candidate use cases; explicit "no" list |
| 2 | Criteria and ranking | 2.5h | Leadership | Agreed weights; scored, ranked portfolio; the pilot |
| 3 | Pilot success criteria | 2h | Owner, team lead, two users, IT, DPO | Error budget; what a person always sees; review screen; kill criteria |
| 4 | Roadmap and operating model | 3h | Leadership (+ employee representatives for the last hour) | Waves, gates, owners (RACI), the ask for the board |

**Workshop 1 agenda:** what AI is good and bad at, with demos on the client's own material (30') · discovery themes with numbers (20') · "How might we...?" silent writing per theme (50') · affinity clustering (40') · dot voting "would you fund it from your own budget?" (20') · what we will not do and why (20').

**Workshop 2 agenda:** why criteria first (15') · 100-point split over value / feasibility / risk, discuss outliers, settle (30') · challenge the pre-filled scores line by line (60') · the matrix (20') · live re-weighting: does the ranking survive? (15') · choose the pilot (10').

**Workshop 3 agenda:** real failure examples from the discovery sample (20') · error budget anchored on today's human error rate (30') · rules for what a person always sees (30') · paper prototype of the review screen with users (25') · kill criteria (15').

**Workshop 4 agenda:** pilot results, live (30') · business case, cash and capacity, finance challenges (30') · sequence waves on the wall using readiness gaps (45') · RACI together (30') · employee representatives join: what changes for people (35') · the board ask (10').

**Facilitation rules:** one decision per workshop, written on the wall at the start · numbers before opinions · the most senior person speaks last on scores · park, do not debate, out-of-scope items · send the decision log within 24 hours.
