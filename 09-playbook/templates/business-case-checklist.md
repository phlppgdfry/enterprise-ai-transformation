# Template: AI business case checklist

- [ ] **Baseline measured**, not estimated: volume, handling time, error rate (sample size stated)
- [ ] **Cash view and capacity view** separated; the cash view names the people decision behind it (hiring avoided, attrition, redeployment) and is agreed with HR and employee representatives
- [ ] **Pilot results feed the case** directly (straight-through rate, error rate), with their confidence interval as the low/high range
- [ ] **Error cost** included as a separate benefit line, with a wide range if estimated
- [ ] **Run cost** includes model usage (tokens × price × volume), hosting, monitoring and upkeep; not just the licence
- [ ] **Ramp-up** in year 1 (go-live date, document types or teams switched on one by one)
- [ ] **Pilot cost treated as sunk**
- [ ] **Scenarios:** pessimistic puts every driver on its bad side at once; shown even if it loses money
- [ ] **Tornado:** which drivers move the NPV most; management attention follows the top three
- [ ] **Kill criteria** per gate, agreed with finance
- [ ] **Soft benefits listed but excluded** from the numbers
- [ ] **Every number has a source label** (measured / pilot / client finance / estimate / assumption)
