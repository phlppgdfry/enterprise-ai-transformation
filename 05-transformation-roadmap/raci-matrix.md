## Three choices worth pointing out

- **The COO, not IT, is accountable for UC-01.** It is an operations change with a technical component, not the other way round. IT is accountable for the platform and for changes to prompts, models and thresholds.
- **The doc desk lead is responsible for the error budget, the COO accountable.** The people who see the errors every day set and watch the threshold; the COO carries the consequence.
- **The partner team is never accountable.** Consultants are responsible for work and consulted on decisions; ownership stays with Wendelkaai so the company can carry on without us.
