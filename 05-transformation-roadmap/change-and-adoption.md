# Change and adoption

The technology is the easy half. The pilot reached its targets in the evaluation; whether it reaches them on the floor depends on eleven clerks trusting it, a works council that was burned in 2023, and a CFO who wants to see euros.

## What people need, per group

| Group | What they fear or doubt | What we do | How we know it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doc desk clerks | Losing their job; being blamed for the machine's errors | Written commitment on jobs; co-designed review queue; clear rule: you answer for what you were asked to check; weekly numbers shared | Review queue used daily; re-keying of straight-through documents drops to zero; pulse survey |
| Doc desk lead | Losing control over quality | Product owner role; owns the threshold with the COO; Monday retro | Retro actions closed; the lead presents results at steering |
| Works council | Monitoring of employees; being told late | Informed and consulted at weeks 1, 14 and 30; UC-08 parked publicly; no individual performance data from the review queue | No objections raised after go-live |
| Office staff | "AI is not for me" or "I already use ChatGPT, so what?" | Literacy tier 1 for everyone; one sanctioned assistant; clear do's and don'ts | Survey: confidence up from 22%; shadow-AI use down |
| CFO | Benefits that never reach the P&L | Cash view; KPI dashboard owned by the CFO; value per gate | Value realised vs plan reported monthly |
| IT team | Another system to look after with six people | One platform; partner support in year 1; runbook | Incidents and change lead time on the platform |

## The commitments made to staff (in writing, week 1)

1. No redundancies because of AI in the roadmap's scope.
2. Freed capacity goes to work the desk has no time for: customs files, exceptions, customer questions.
3. Leavers are reviewed one by one; while the solution scales, every second one is not replaced.
4. The review queue records what was decided, not how fast each clerk works. Its data is not used to evaluate individuals.
5. Clerks co-design every change to the review flow.

## Measuring adoption

| Indicator | Target by week 26 |
|---|---|
| Straight-through documents re-keyed by hand | 0 |
| Share of flagged fields confirmed or corrected within the same day | 95% |
| Clerks who say the reasons on a flag are useful (pulse survey) | 80% |
| Staff who completed literacy tier 1 | 90% |
| Staff who say they use public chatbots for work | below 10% |
