# Client brief

> *"Our board wants an AI plan by the end of the year. Competitors say they use AI. Where do we start, what is it worth, and how do we do it without getting into trouble?"*
> — CEO, Wendelkaai Logistics, first call

**Wendelkaai Logistics NV is fictional.** The company, people, figures and documents in this repository are invented for a portfolio case. The regulations, standards and methods are real.

## The company

| | |
|---|---|
| What | Container and ro-ro forwarding, customs brokerage, warehousing, road transport |
| Where | Zeebrugge (Brugge), Belgium; two warehouses, 40,000 m² |
| Size | 250 employees, €78m revenue, family-owned (second generation) |
| Systems | On-premise TMS (2014) with an import API, finance ERP (2016), Microsoft 365 with Entra ID, customs declaration software, shared mailboxes, a lot of Excel |
| Certifications | ISO 9001; ISO/IEC 27001:2022 for IT services and customs data (since 2024); AEO-F |
| Teams that matter here | Doc desk (11 FTE), customer service (7), customs (9), IT (6) |

## What the leadership team told us in the first week

- **Hiring.** The doc desk has had two open vacancies for eight months. Volume grows about 4% a year.
- **Errors.** Keying errors show up later as customs corrections, rework and demurrage. Nobody knew how often until we measured it.
- **Shadow AI.** Staff use public chatbots for work. Some paste customer data into them. There is no policy.
- **Vendor noise.** Three vendors pitched "AI" in 2026: a warehouse productivity tool, a TMS add-on and a chatbot. None was compared with the others.
- **Pressure from customers.** Two pharma customers asked in their 2026 supplier audits how Wendelkaai governs AI.

## What success looks like (agreed in week 1)

1. The board gets a plan it can decide on: where to start, what it is worth, what it costs and what could go wrong.
2. One use case is proven on real-looking documents before the board meets, not just on slides.
3. Nothing in the plan creates a legal, security or works-council problem.
4. The company can carry on without the consultants: owners, a policy and a way to choose the next use case.

## Scope

**In scope:** the whole company for discovery and readiness; one pilot.
**Out of scope:** replacing the TMS, customs software, and anything that monitors individual employees (see [UC-08](../03-opportunity-discovery/use-case-catalog.md)).
